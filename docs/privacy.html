Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Her is an open-source Android assistant. This page explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The app has no server of its own: the developer does not receive, store, or see your conversations or your data.

Data stored on your phone

Your conversation with Her, and what she organizes from it (memories, tasks, groceries, people, dates, reminders and similar records), is stored in a database on your phone. Your AI provider API key is kept in encrypted storage backed by the Android Keystore. It is never written to the database or to a backup file.

Your AI provider

Her works with an AI model that you choose and configure, using your own API key (any OpenAI-compatible service). To answer you, the app sends the relevant parts of your conversation and records to that provider. When calendar access is on, this can include events from your phone's calendar that are relevant to your request. When contacts access is on and you ask about someone, this can include that contact's name, phone numbers and birthday; Her does not upload your contact list. That provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to what it receives. If you turn on web search, your search queries, and the country and time zone from your profile, are sent to the same provider.

Backups

Settings → Backup can save a zip file of your Her database and settings to a place you choose, or hand it to an app you pick. Her never uploads it anywhere by itself. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private. Your API keys are never in it. Importing a backup replaces the data on your phone with what is in the file.

What Her does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No account with the developer, and no developer server.
  • No sale or sharing of your data.

Your control

You can look at and delete what Her remembers from inside the app, and turn calendar or contacts access off in Settings at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes its data from your phone. Backup files you saved stay where you put them until you delete them.

Earlier versions could connect a Google account. Her no longer does. If you connected one, you can revoke Her's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove its old Drive backup in Google Drive's settings under “Manage apps”.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/AlirezaAzadbakht/her/issues.
