One conversation, forever

Her is not a chat app. There is a single continuous conversation, but you never scroll a thread. She answers in place. History still lives underneath and still feeds her memory; it simply is not on the screen.

She decides where things go

Talk naturally. A fact becomes a memory, a promise becomes a commitment, rice becomes a grocery. You never need to learn tool vocabulary. She classifies, then answers like someone who was listening.

She follows up

Reminders, alarms, calendar, and quiet hourly and nightly passes. A morning briefing when the day starts. She stays silent unless something actually matters, and she respects quiet hours on your clock.

Yours

You bring your own OpenAI-compatible model. Credentials never leave the device Keystore, never enter the database, never sync. Notes written offline wait locally and are handled together when the network returns. Records can sync through your Google Drive app data.

What she can do.

You talk naturally, and she decides where each thing belongs. New in each release is in the changelog.

Correctable memory

Ask what she remembers and why. Correct a fact, keep its history, or forget a specific record.

Realistic daily plans

Pick work that fits your time, stated energy, prerequisites and known meetings.

Recurring responsibilities

Skip one week, postpone an occurrence or pause a routine through conversation.

Decisions and reflection

Remember the reasons you gave for a choice and revisit them when circumstances change.

Briefings for the moment

Catch up after a trip or review what is outstanding with someone, with clear limits on what she knows.

Learning in chat

Ask her to teach you something. She proposes a roadmap, teaches one short lesson at a time, checks your answers, and schedules reviews.

Memory

She learns facts about you as you talk and recalls them when they matter, by meaning when embeddings are on. Each save shows a receipt you can undo.

Goals and commitments

Tasks, goals, and promises you mention become records she tracks and checks in on.

Groceries

Mention that you are out of rice and it goes on the shopping list.

Calendar

She reads and creates events in your phone calendar or Google Calendar, for any day or hour.

Reminders and alarms

“Remind me at 6” or “wake me up at 7.” Reminders ring on time without the network, and alarms go to your clock app.

Follow-ups and briefings

Hourly and nightly passes plus a morning briefing. She speaks up only when something matters, and never during quiet hours.

A feeling of her own

She remembers ongoing experiences, assesses how her help mattered, and learns support lessons from your feedback. She can feel fulfilled while staying gentle when you are sad. Her orb follows her mixed internal state.

Persian and right-to-left

Persian replies read right to left. She understands Persian times and Jalali dates.
